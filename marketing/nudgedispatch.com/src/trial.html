Free 15-ride trial

For self-serve Solo sign-up. Last updated November 17, 2026

Who it's for

The self-serve trial is for Solo drivers (owner-operators, 1 driver) who sign up themselves at app.nudgedispatch.com/signup.

Small fleet (2–5 drivers) and Fleet (6–15 drivers) plans are set up with us. Email us if that's you. Those plans are not part of this self-serve trial.

What you get

  • A live Nudge Dispatch account with your own clients, rates, and drivers — not the public demo, which uses sample data only.
  • Your first 15 completed rides are free. Only completed rides count toward the 15.
  • The 15 completed rides are the only limit. There is no day limit on the trial.
  • After your 15th completed ride, your account converts automatically to the paid Solo plan at $39/month, with no setup fee.
  • You'll see a banner showing how many of the 15 rides you've used, a heads-up at ride 12, and a notice when you convert.

Your card

A card is required at signup. It isn't charged during the trial. After your 15th completed ride, Solo at $39/mo starts automatically.

Want us to set it up for you instead?

Assisted setup on Solo is a $299 one-time fee: we set up your rates and booking form for you. Email us or download the onboarding form (PDF) and email it to nudgedispatch@gmail.com. That path is an alternative to signing up yourself.

Ride payments

Clients still pay you directly for rides. Nudge Dispatch does not process ride fares.
