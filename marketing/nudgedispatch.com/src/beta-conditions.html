The beta program has ended

Updated November 17, 2026

The Nudge Dispatch beta tester program, including the 3-spot offer, the 15-ride or 30-day cap, the “no obligation to buy” trial, and the BETA50 setup-fee discount, is no longer offered.

Solo drivers can now sign up themselves for a 15-ride free trial. Your first 15 completed rides are free. A card is required at signup; it isn't charged during the trial. After your 15th completed ride, your account converts automatically to Solo at $39/mo, with no setup fee. The 15 rides are the only limit.
