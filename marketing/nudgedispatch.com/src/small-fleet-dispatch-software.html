The three moments that eat a small fleet's day

“Did anyone answer that website request?”

A booking request sits in your texts until the client books someone else.

With Nudge: Every request from your website or booking link lands in one approval queue. Approve or decline it in one tap.

“Who's closest to the airport?”

The flight lands in 25 minutes and you're calling three drivers to find out where they are.

With Nudge: Drivers who are on duty show up ranked by distance to the pickup, with a one-tap flight link.

“Invoices at midnight, again.”

The day's done, and now you're retyping trips into invoices.

With Nudge: The invoice is ready when the ride is done, with your own logo. You copy or download it. Nothing is sent automatically.

What a small fleet gets
