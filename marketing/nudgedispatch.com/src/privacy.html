Nudge Dispatch — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026
Website: nudgedispatch.com
Service: Nudge Dispatch — dispatch and booking software for independent car-service operators, drivers, and small fleets
Operated by: Iron Gate Executive Transport LLC, Georgia, USA
Contact: nudgedispatch@gmail.com

Nudge Dispatch is operated by Iron Gate Executive Transport LLC, a Georgia, USA company ("Nudge Dispatch," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle when you use nudgedispatch.com, app.nudgedispatch.com, company booking pages, and the Nudge Dispatch app (together, the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Who This Covers

Nudge Dispatch is software used by car-service businesses, from solo owner-operators to small fleets (each a "company"), to run their own rides, clients, and drivers. Each company controls the information it enters or imports about its clients and drivers, and we handle that information on the company's behalf to run the Service for it. If you are a passenger or client of a company that uses Nudge Dispatch, that company is responsible for how it uses your information, and you can contact it directly.

2. Information We Handle

Information companies and users provide

  • Account information — name, business name, email address, phone number, logo, rates, and sign-in details for owners, dispatchers, and drivers.
  • Driver information — driver names, phone numbers, vehicle type and plate, and sign-in passcodes, as entered by the company.
  • Ride and client information — client and passenger names, phone numbers, email addresses, pickup and drop-off places, dates and times, flight numbers, passenger counts, and ride notes, whether entered by the company, imported by it, or sent through its booking page.
  • Payment status and invoice information — prices, whether a ride or invoice has been paid, and related billing records the company keeps in the Service. We do not process ride payments and we do not store card numbers.
  • Imported data — records a company brings over from a spreadsheet (such as Google Sheets) or another system, as described in Section 4.
  • Communications — messages you send us by email or through the Service, including support and setup requests.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical and log data — such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, and error and request logs kept by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, to keep the Service running and secure.
  • Cookies and browser storage — used to keep you signed in and remember basic settings. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but the Service may not work properly without them.

3. Driver and Owner Location Sharing

  • It's opt-in. Location is shared only when a driver (or the owner) turns on location sharing while on duty in the app and allows location in the browser. It stops when sharing is turned off, the person signs out, or the phone is locked or the app is closed.
  • Only the latest position. We keep one position per person, the most recent one, and overwrite it with each update (about every 45 seconds while on duty).
  • It expires after 15 minutes. If no new update arrives, the position is deleted automatically after 15 minutes.
  • No history. We don't keep a trail, route history, or log of past positions.
  • Who sees it. Only the owner and dispatchers of the same company. Drivers never see each other's positions, and other companies never see yours.
  • Nothing automatic. Distances are straight-line estimates used to suggest the closest driver. Nobody is assigned a ride automatically. A person at the company always decides who gets a ride.

4. Data Import, Migration, and Setup Help

Companies can bring their existing records into Nudge Dispatch, for example clients, rides, drivers, and invoices kept in a spreadsheet such as Google Sheets or in another system. Setup and testing happen on the company's real data, not sample data.

  • What we import. Only the data the company chooses to share with us for import. The company is responsible for having the right to share it.
  • Setup help from our staff. When a company asks for help with setup or migration, a small number of Nudge Dispatch staff may access that company's data, and any spreadsheet or export it shares with us, only to set up its account, move and check its data, and provide support. We don't use it for anything else.
  • Connected Google Sheets. If a company connects its own Google Sheet, that company's ride and client data may also be stored in its Google Sheet. That sheet stays in the company's own Google account and is also governed by Google's terms and the company's sharing settings.
  • If a company leaves. A company can ask for an export of its data at any time, and can ask us to delete its data, including imported data, by emailing nudgedispatch@gmail.com. We'll confirm when the deletion is done. Copies the company keeps in its own spreadsheets or systems are under its control.
  • Free trial. During the self-serve Solo trial, the company may store real client and ride information in the Service (including names, phone numbers, email addresses, pickup and drop-off addresses, flight numbers, and opt-in driver location while on duty). The company is the party responsible for its own clients' and drivers' information and for having the right to enter or import it. The trial covers 15 completed rides and does not have a day limit. After the 15th completed ride, the account converts to the paid Solo plan and the company's data remains in the account. If the company closes the account during the trial, or later closes a paid account, the company can ask for an export and can ask us to delete its data, including imported data, by emailing nudgedispatch@gmail.com. We will delete it as described in Section 9, except anonymized data that does not identify the company, its drivers, its clients, or anyone else.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including booking pages, ride and dispatch tools, driver tools, location-based suggestions, payment-status tracking, and invoices
  • Set up accounts, import and migrate a company's data, and help companies during onboarding
  • Send account holders messages about their account and the Service
  • Send text messages as described in Section 7, where the company has turned this on and the recipient has consented
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve the Service and develop new features
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. What We Don't Do, and How We Share Information

  • We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.
  • We don't process ride payments or store card numbers. Clients pay the company directly.
  • The app doesn't email or message a company's clients on its own. The only exception is text messages the company has turned on and the recipient has agreed to receive (see Section 7). Otherwise, invoices and messages are prepared for the company to send itself.

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Within a company's account — ride and client information is visible to that company's owner, its dispatchers, and, for a given ride, the driver assigned to it.
  • Service providers — vendors that help us run the Service, such as our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and a messaging provider that delivers text messages. They may use information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Nudge Dispatch, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of assets, subject to confidentiality protections.

7. Text Messaging (SMS)

Consent

By providing a mobile phone number and opting in through the Service, a booking page, or another Nudge Dispatch opt-in method, you consent to receive text messages from Nudge Dispatch and from the company using Nudge Dispatch for your booking. These messages may include booking confirmations, pickup and ride updates, driver notices, and account-related messages. Text messages are sent only when the company has turned them on.

No sharing of text-messaging consent

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose text-messaging consent or opt-in data to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information and text-messaging consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except as strictly necessary to deliver the text-messaging service itself (for example, to a messaging provider that transmits the messages on our behalf).

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your bookings and account activity. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opting out

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to a message. After you opt out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further text messages, except where a message is required by law. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at nudgedispatch@gmail.com.

Opting out of text messages may affect your ability to receive ride updates for a booking.

8. Where Data Is Stored

The Service is hosted on Cloudflare in the United States and other locations where Cloudflare operates. If a company connects its own Google Sheet, that company's ride and client data may also be stored in its Google Sheet.

9. Keeping and Deleting Data

  • A location position is deleted when the person turns sharing off or signs out, or automatically after 15 minutes without an update.
  • Other company data, including imported data, is kept while the company's account is active and is deleted when the company asks us to, or after its account is closed.
  • When a company closes its account, we delete the company's data when the company asks us to, or after a 30-day export/delete window following closure, except anonymized data and records we must keep by law. Conversion from the self-serve trial to a paid Solo plan is not an account closure and does not start that window.
  • Deleted data may remain in routine backups for a limited time before those backups are overwritten.
  • We may keep limited records where the law requires it or to resolve disputes.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, including limiting staff access to what's needed for setup and support. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Access, correct, or update your account information through the Service or by contacting us
  • Ask for a copy or export of your company's data
  • Ask us to delete your information by emailing nudgedispatch@gmail.com
  • Turn location sharing off at any time
  • Opt out of text messages by replying STOP, as described above

If you are a client or passenger of a company that uses Nudge Dispatch, please send requests about your information to that company. If you contact us, we'll pass your request to the company or help it respond. We will respond to requests within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law.
