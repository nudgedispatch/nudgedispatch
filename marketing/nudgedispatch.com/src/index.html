The three moments that eat a small fleet's day

“Did anyone answer that website request?”

A booking request sits in your texts until the client books someone else.

With Nudge: Every request from your website or booking link lands in one approval queue. Approve or decline it in one tap.

“Who's closest to the airport?”

The flight lands in 25 minutes and you're calling three drivers to find out where they are.

With Nudge: Drivers who are on duty show up ranked by distance to the pickup, with a one-tap flight link.

“Invoices at midnight, again.”

The day's done, and now you're retyping trips into invoices.

With Nudge: The invoice is ready when the ride is done, with your own logo. You copy or download it. Nothing is sent automatically.

How it works

From request to invoice in 3 steps

  1. 1

    Requests come in

    Your website form or booking link drops each request into the queue: pickup, flight, passengers and bags.

  2. 2

    You approve and assign

    Tap approve, then choose the driver. If drivers are on duty, the closest ones are listed first.

  3. 3

    Ride done, paperwork done

    Mark it paid, unpaid or invoiced, and the invoice with your logo is ready to copy or download.

Questions

Before you ask

Does Nudge Dispatch process payments?

No. Clients still pay you directly. You track unpaid, invoiced and paid for each ride, and the invoice is ready with your logo when the ride is done.

What is the free trial?

Solo drivers can sign up themselves and get 15 free completed rides. A card is required at signup; it isn't charged during the trial. After your 15th completed ride, your account converts automatically to the paid Solo plan at $39/month, with no setup fee. You'll see a banner showing how many of the 15 rides you've used, a heads-up at ride 12, and a notice when you convert. Only completed rides count. The 15 rides are the only limit.

Is the free trial sample data only?

No. The public demo uses sample data only. The free trial is a live account with your own clients and rides. Your first 15 completed rides are free, then your account converts automatically to Solo. Clients still pay you directly for rides.

Is there a setup fee on Solo, and what does it cover?

If you sign up yourself, there is no setup fee. You get 15 free completed rides, then Solo is $39/month. A card is required at signup and isn't charged during the trial. Want us to set it up for you? Assisted setup is $299 one-time: we set up your rates and booking form for you. On Small fleet ($599) and Fleet ($1,199), the one-time setup fee covers getting you set up: your rates and booking form set up for you, and on Fleet, importing from your old system and a training call.

What's in the demo?

Made-up sample data only: sample rides, clients and drivers. You can open it as the owner or as any driver. Nothing in it is real, and nobody is contacted. Your changes stay in your own browser, and Reset demo puts the sample data back.
