Changelog

What's shipped so far, newest first. I'm Jesse Bailey; I run a private chauffeur service in metro Atlanta and I'm building Nudge Dispatch for small fleets like mine. Try any of this in the demo (sample data only).

Nov 17, 2026

  • Self-serve Solo sign-up now starts with a 15-ride free trial. Sign up yourself, take 15 completed rides free, then the account converts automatically to Solo at $39/mo with no setup fee. A card is required at signup and isn't charged during the trial. You'll see a banner with how many of the 15 rides you've used, a heads-up at ride 12, and a notice when you convert. The previous beta offer (3 spots, 15 rides or 30 days, no obligation to buy, 50% off setup with code BETA50) is retired.
  • The small-fleet page now explains the setup fee: with more than one vehicle, we set up each car and driver for you and load your existing clients.
  • The sample-data demo now has a banner inviting you to sign up with your own fleet.
  • Removed the homepage FAQ line that said not to enter card numbers or bank details.

Nov 10, 2026

  • Setup fees set at Solo $299, Small fleet $599 and Fleet $1,199. Beta testers who stay on after the free trial get 50% off the setup fee (code BETA50).
  • New contact address for Nudge Dispatch: nudgedispatch@gmail.com.

Nov 9, 2026

  • Beta offer updated: 3 spots, free live trial with your own data, capped at 15 completed rides or 30 days, whichever comes first. Beta conditions, Terms, and Privacy updated to match. The public demo remains sample data only.

Nov 3, 2026

  • nudgedispatch.com is live, with pricing and plain-language privacy and terms pages.
  • New in the demo: opt-in driver location. Turn on “On duty, share my location” and each upcoming ride lists the nearest drivers by distance. It's a suggestion only; you still choose who gets the ride. In the demo the locations are made-up sample spots.

Nov 2, 2026

  • New in the demo: invoices with your own logo, name and payment details on completed rides. Copy the text, print it, or download a PDF. Nothing is sent for you.

Oct 29, 2026

  • The demo opened at demo.nudgedispatch.com with sample data only: the ride list, the Needs your approval queue with Approve and Decline, payment status and clients.
  • Flight tracking links now show on pending, confirmed and completed airport rides.
  • Driver assignment and a driver view where each driver sees only their own rides, plus a booking page for riders.
