# Nudge Dispatch

Dispatch and booking software for small car-service fleets. Built by operators, for operators.

## What it does

- One screen for the whole job — approval queue, driver assignment, payment status, and invoices with your logo
- A booking link your customers keep on their phone
- Area-based quick quotes — customers can counter, drivers approve, decline, or schedule
- Opt-in nearest-driver location and flight links for airport runs
